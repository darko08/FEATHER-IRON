Story

The spring the Farmer didn't come

For generations the Farmer opened the gate at dawn, poured the grain and collected the eggs. Every coop on the Estate had an address, and every Chikn had its place in the Pecking Order.

One spring the gate stayed shut. In the farmhouse kitchen the Chikn found the Farmer's notebook on hatching. It taught them that an egg can hatch whatever the flock needs: a soldier, a pack hen, a wall.

The old coops by the farmhouse claimed the notebook and the full silo. The coops along the fence refused. The night the shared feeder was kicked over, the war for the Pecking Order began.

What they fight over

Grain

The big silo is full, and every building and every soldier eats grain.

Eggs

The only thing on the Estate that can become anything. Whoever holds eggs holds an army.

Meadow plots

The Pecking Order

Rank used to be settled by the Farmer. Now it is taken.

The ticket

Every Chikn is a seat in the world

You play by holding a Chikn NFT. Each NFT opens one account with one home coop and brings a fixed stock of eggs into your wallet.

One NFT, one account

Connect a wallet and pick a Chikn. It becomes the account's ticket, and its home coop lands on a free Meadow or Gardens plot.

The account follows the NFT

Sell or transfer the NFT and the account moves with it: coops, flocks and research.

One seat per wallet

A wallet plays one account in a world at a time. Extra Chikn are spare tickets to sell or lend.

Eggs ride in the wallet

Eggs are a token. They can be traded, nobody can raid them, and using one in the game burns it.

The one open number

How many eggs does each Chikn start with?

We recommend 1,000. That is about a week of early production: you feel it on day one, and by day 30 an active player produces as much in under a day.

What the starting eggs buy

New coops start with a level 3 Hatchery. Each card is one way to spend all the eggs, so the cards don't add up.

Storage and raids

  • Granary, Grit Bin and Oil Tank cap what a coop can hold. Production stops when storage is full.
  • A raid takes up to 40% of each stored resource, limited by the raiders' cargo.
  • Eggs are never in a coop, so no raid can reach them.
  • Exchange value: Grain, Grit and Oil trade at 1 : 2 : 4.

World

Coops instead of planets

Each account starts with one coop on one plot. The plot works like a planet: its place on the street sets how many fields you can build on and how hot it is.

1 worldone server, 32,000 seats
12 neighborhoodsin a ring around the farmhouse
500 streetsin each neighborhood
15 plotsper street, from the road to the grove

Addresses read like a village address: Sunflower 117/6 is the Sunflower neighborhood, street 117, plot 6. A trip along the same street takes minutes, across a neighborhood hours, and to another neighborhood most of a day. 90,000 plots in total.

Game systems

Build, research, hatch, raid

The loop is the one players know from the browser strategy classics: mines feed buildings and research, the Hatchery turns resources and eggs into units, and flocks travel to raid, trade and settle. Names, numbers and balance below are our own.

Production

  • Grain Field · Grain
  • Grit Pit · Grit
  • Oil Press · Oil
  • Windmill · Power
  • Compost Furnace · Power from Oil

Storage

  • Granary · holds Grain
  • Grit Bin · holds Grit
  • Oil Tank · holds Oil

Facilities

  • Henhouse · main building, faster construction
  • Workshop · faster hatching, unlocks heavy units
  • Hatchery · units, defense and egg use
  • Academy · research
  • Fence Extension · +4 fields per level
  • Watchtower · spots incoming flocks earlier
  • Feeder Depot · feeds allied flocks on guard

Research

  • Beak Sharpening · +5% attack per level
  • Shell Plating · +5% health
  • Down Padding · +2% armor, up to +20%
  • Long Stride, Pack Training, Heavy March · speed for light, pack and heavy units
  • Scouting · fuller reports
  • Cartography · +1 coop every 2 levels
  • Agronomy · +2% production
  • Egg Genetics · +3% units per egg
  • Logistics · +1 flock in the field

Combat

  • A battle lasts up to 6 rounds. Each round, every unit strikes one random enemy.
  • Damage = Attack × (1 − target Armor). A Shell Dome soaks damage first, up to its capacity each round.
  • Frenzy: some units strike again after hitting their favorite target. Peckers chase Scouts, Egg Mortars hammer Slinger Nests and Corn Cannons, Breachers smash domes and towers.
  • 35% of the Grain and Grit in destroyed units falls as a feather field over the plot.
  • 60% of destroyed defenses rebuild themselves after the battle.

Protection

Fledgling shield

New accounts can't be attacked for 7 days. The shield drops early if you raid a player.

Molting

Holiday mode, 2 to 30 days. Production stops and nobody can attack you or be attacked by you.

Inactive tags

(i) after 7 days, (I) after 28. Inactive coops can be raided, and the account stays with its NFT.

Alliances

Joint Raids of up to four players, Guard missions for allies, and a shared ranking in the Pecking Order.
